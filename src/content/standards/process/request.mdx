## When to submit

- Twice a year, during the periodic review, OCHA or your agency asks focal points to check their country's boundaries. Submit one response for each country you cover.
- Submit at any other time when boundaries change, or when a new or improved dataset is needed, for example in an emergency.
- Submit even if nothing has changed. Answering "No" to the question about changes confirms that the current dataset is still correct.

## Who submits

The country's COD focal point is the main respondent. OCHA country, HAT and regional offices and UN80 agencies can also submit for the same country. When there are several submissions, OCHA ISS reviews them and reconciles any differences.

## Before you start

1. Check your country's current boundaries in the [Boundaries Explorer](../../../explorer/).
2. Find out which admin levels have a candidate dataset, and whether each one is up to date.
3. If something has changed, prepare a short description of what changed and any caveats.

## What the form asks

| Question | What to give |
| :---- | :---- |
| Country | The country this response is about |
| Submitting organisation or office | Your OCHA office or UN80 agency |
| Role | Your responsibility for COD-AB in this country |
| Changes | Yes, No or Planned |
| Description | What changed or will change, if Yes or Planned |
| Candidate dataset | For each admin level (1 to 5): up to date, outdated, partial, not available or planned |
| Expected date | When a dataset will be available, if it isn't yet |
| Challenges | Up to three reasons a dataset isn't available |
| Comments | Anything else, including why your answer differs from other submissions |

The form doesn't record your name or email address. If you want someone to follow up with you, add your contact details in the comments.

## What happens next

OCHA ISS reviews every submission. If a change is needed, the update starts at [Step 2: Initialization](../steps/#step-2-initialization).
